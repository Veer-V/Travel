# SmartVoyage AI — Traveler User Guide

Welcome to **SmartVoyage AI**, your intelligent mobile travel companion designed for discovering, planning, and navigating trips across India with personalized machine learning recommendations, evidence-based safety indicators, and transparent budget forecasting.

---

## 1. Getting Started
- **Guest Browsing**: You can explore all 56 destinations, browse points of interest, examine safety advisories, and create travel itineraries immediately without registration.
- **Account Registration**: Create a free account under the **Profile** tab to synchronize your saved trips, set dietary and accessibility preferences, and log actual expenses.

---

## 2. Planning a Personalized Trip
1. Tap the **Plan Trip** tab on the bottom navigation bar.
2. Enter your **Departure City** (or select a quick hub like Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru).
3. Select your **Target Destination** from the catalog of 56 curated regions.
4. Specify your travel dates and group size.
5. Set your target **Budget** in INR (₹).
6. Pick your **Travel Style** (`Heritage`, `Beach`, `Hills`, `Adventure`, `Spiritual`, etc.).
7. Specify transit preferences (`Train`, `Flight`, `Bus`, `Drive`), lodging tier (`Hostel`, `Hotel`, `Resort`), and accessibility needs (`Standard`, `Wheelchair`, `Step-Free`).
8. Tap **Generate Personalized Travel Plan**.

---

## 3. Navigating Your AI Itinerary
- **Day-by-Day Timeline**: Tap across Day 1, Day 2, Day 3 tabs to inspect morning activities, lunch breaks at authentic regional eateries, afternoon exploration, sunset viewpoints, and evening dining.
- **Activity Badges**: Each stop displays estimated visit duration, admission fees, accessibility features, and why our AI recommended it.
- **Route Map**: Tap **Route Map** in the header banner to inspect the geographic route connecting all scheduled stops.
- **Safety Dashboard**: Tap **Safety Dashboard** to review environmental hazards (monsoon, heatwaves), petty crime frequencies, official government advisories, and 24/7 emergency hotlines.
- **Budget Breakdown**: Tap **Budget Details** to analyze itemized cost allocations and log actual expenses on the go.
- **Sharing**: Tap the share icon in the top right to share your itinerary summary with family or travel companions.

---

## 4. Understanding Safety Ratings
SmartVoyage AI utilizes an evidence-based status system:
- **Lower observed risk**: Low historical incident rates and standard conditions.
- **Moderate observed risk**: Typical pedestrian crowd density or minor seasonal weather advisory.
- **Elevated observed risk**: Severe weather hazard (e.g. heavy monsoon landslide advisory in mountain passes).
- **Active official warning**: Active government advisory recommending avoiding non-essential transit.
- **Insufficient data**: Incomplete or stale records trigger this caution flag rather than false reassurance.

> **Emergency Note**: In any emergency in India, dial **112** for Police/Fire/Rescue, **108** for Ambulance, or **1363** for the Ministry of Tourism Multi-lingual Helpline.
